About, sources and disclaimer

Blockchain, Hands On · published by Usualy Labs · version 1.0.0 · All tools

What this is

A teaching model that runs entirely in your browser. The keys, signatures and SHA-256 fingerprints are real cryptography, but the chain, the network and the people are simulated on this page. It is not connected to Bitcoin, Ethereum or any other network, and no real money or cryptoassets are involved. Real blockchains add much more (peer-to-peer messaging, fees, difficulty adjustment, scripts and different signature schemes).

Your privacy

Usualy Labs collects nothing and this page makes no network requests: no accounts, analytics, cookies, trackers or web fonts. Keys are created by your browser's built-in Web Crypto and are thrown away when you close the page. Only your theme choice is remembered, in your own browser.
