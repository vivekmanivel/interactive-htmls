# UK Take-Home Pay & Contracting Calculator

A free, single-file calculator for UK take-home pay and contractor comparisons, tax year 2026/27.

## What it does
- **Employee**: Income Tax (rest of UK or Scotland), National Insurance, salary sacrifice / net pay / relief-at-source pensions, employer pension, two student loans, benefits in kind, Child Benefit charge, Marriage Allowance; per year, month or week.
- **Contractor**: limited company (salary + dividends, Corporation Tax, expenses, employer pension, extraction level) vs umbrella vs a permanent role.
- Every tax figure can be overridden.
- Share link, export/import, print, theme switcher (auto, light, dark).

## Data sources
HMRC / GOV.UK 2026/27 rates and thresholds, Scottish income tax bands, Student Loans Company thresholds, GOV.UK IR35 guidance. Day rates and expenses are illustrative. Full credits are in the About section.

## Privacy
No network requests. Inputs are stored only in your browser.

## Limitations
- Figures last checked October 2026.
- IR35 status isn't assessed; it depends on each contract's facts (see HMRC's CEST tool).

## Disclaimer
Illustrations only, not tax, accounting or financial advice.
