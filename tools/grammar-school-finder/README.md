# UK Grammar School Finder

A free, single-file guide to every state grammar school in England and Northern Ireland.

## What it does
| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Map & search | Type a postcode or drop a pin; schools within your chosen drive time or distance, reach and coverage layers, catchment of a selected school |
| Schools & compare | Search, filter and sort all grammar schools; compare side by side |
| School profile | GCSE and A-level results, admissions demand and last offers, leavers' destinations, Ofsted, catchment rules with source links |
| Rankings your way | Weight the measures that matter to you |
| Insights | What the data shows across all schools |
| Tests & key dates | Test areas and deadlines (including SEAG in Northern Ireland) |
| My report | Print-ready summary |
| Data & methods | Sources, how reach and rankings work, limits |
| References & credits | Every source URL (council and school admission arrangements for all 163 English grammars), credits, licences, privacy and disclaimer |

Plus share link, export/import, print and an Auto / Light / Dark theme.

## Data sources
DfE (Get Information about Schools, KS4 and 16-19 performance, destination measures, applications and offers), Ofsted management information, DE NI and the Education Authority NI, SEAG, and each school's determined admission arrangements from councils and schools. Postcode lookups use postcodes.io; maps use Esri and OpenStreetMap tiles. The full list with links is in the tool's **References & credits** tab.

## Privacy
Nothing is sent to Usualy Labs. Postcode lookups go from your browser to postcodes.io, and map tiles load from Esri or OpenStreetMap; those services see your IP address and the postcode or map area. Inputs are stored only in your browser.

## Limitations
- Catchment rules are for September 2027 entry where published (some older ones are flagged). Policies change every year.
- Some areas (faith, feeder schools) can't be mapped exactly; these are described in words.
- Catchment shading for list-based areas is approximate.

## Disclaimer
Information only, not educational, legal or admissions advice. Always check the school's and council's own arrangements. Provided as is, with no liability accepted.
