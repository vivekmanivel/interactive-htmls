# Debt Payoff Planner

A free, single-file planner for clearing several debts at once. Works in any currency; examples are UK-based.

## What it does
- Enter each debt: balance, APR, minimum payment rule, promotional rate.
- Choose **avalanche** (highest rate first), **snowball** (smallest balance first) or your own order.
- Month-by-month schedule, debt-free date, total interest, and how much an extra amount or lump sum saves.
- Checks whether a balance transfer or consolidation loan would actually help.
- Share link, export/import, print, theme switcher (auto, light, dark).

## Data sources
General repayment methods; UK minimum-payment examples follow FCA consumer credit rules. All rates and balances are illustrative. Full credits are in the tool's About section.

## Privacy
No network requests. Inputs are stored only in your browser.

## Free debt help
MoneyHelper, StepChange, National Debtline and Citizens Advice give free, confidential debt advice.
