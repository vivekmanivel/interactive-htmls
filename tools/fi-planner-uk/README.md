# Financial Independence Planner (UK)

A free, single-file planner for working out when you could stop working, using UK accounts and tax.

## What it does
- Projects cash, general investment account, ISA, Lifetime ISA and pension pots with contributions, returns, fees and inflation.
- Retirement spending with a slow-down phase, mortgage end date and one-off costs.
- Drawdown order across accounts with UK income tax, capital gains, 25% tax-free pension cash, pension access age and State Pension.
- Monte Carlo simulation showing how often the plan lasts, plus safe-withdrawal-rate checks.
- Share link, export/import, print, theme switcher (auto, light, dark).

## Data sources
HMRC 2026/27 tax and NI; GOV.UK ISA, LISA and pension rules; DWP State Pension. Returns, inflation and volatility are editable assumptions. Full credits are in the About section.

## Privacy
No network requests. Inputs are stored only in your browser.

## Limitations
- Tax figures last checked October 2026. Pension and ISA rules change.
- Simulations are not forecasts.

## Disclaimer
Illustrations only, not financial, investment, pension or tax advice. For free pension guidance, see Pension Wise (MoneyHelper).
