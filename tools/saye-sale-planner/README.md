# SAYE Sale Planner

A free, single-file planner for working out the UK Capital Gains Tax on selling Sharesave (SAYE) shares, and how many to move to a spouse or civil partner so both annual exemptions are used.

**Open it:** `index.html`, or the link on the main README. No install, no account.

## What it does
- Gives an answer first: how to sell with no CGT if that is possible, and the CGT due if it isn't.
- Slider for shares moved to a spouse, with the tax-free range shown in green.
- Compares four ways to sell: all yourself, split with a spouse, spread over two tax years, or split and spread.
- Line-by-line breakdown: proceeds, cost, dealing fee, gain, exemption, tax at each rate and cash left.
- Shows how much of each person's annual exemption is used.
- Checks whether the cash fits in each person's remaining ISA allowance.
- Rates and allowances can be edited. Theme switcher (auto, light, dark).

## Data sources
GOV.UK and HMRC guidance: Capital Gains Tax allowances and rates, Income Tax bands and Personal Allowance, gifts to a spouse, share matching rules, Sharesave, ISAs and Self Assessment deadlines. Links are in the tool's **About, data sources and disclaimer** section. Figures shown when the page opens are made-up examples.

## Privacy
No network requests. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. Only your theme choice is remembered, in your own browser.

## Limitations
- Rates last checked **November 2026**. 2027/28 and later assume today's rules.
- Assumes one holding at one average cost per person, and that the transfer to a spouse is a valid outright gift.
- Doesn't model Scottish income tax differences on income, reporting thresholds beyond the exemption, or tax-advantaged scheme transfers into an ISA.
- Selling in a later tax year depends on the share price change you enter.
