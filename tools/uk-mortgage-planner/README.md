# UK Mortgage Planner

A free, single-file planner that follows a UK mortgage from first affordability check to the day it is paid off. Everything updates as you type and every tab shares the same inputs.

## What it does
| Step | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Profile & affordability | Take-home pay after tax, NI, pension and student loan; a lender-style view of how much you could borrow and which limit binds |
| Costs & stamp duty | SDLT for first-time buyers, movers and additional homes; every upfront cost; cash needed vs cash you have; time to save each deposit size |
| Mortgage plan | Repayment, interest-only or part-and-part; fixed, tracker, discount or SVR; remortgages at LTV-band rates; overpayments, lump sums, ERCs, offset; full schedule (CSV export) |
| Compare deals | Up to six deals side by side: true cost after fees and cashback, base-rate shocks |
| Overpay or invest | Finds the yearly overpayment that maximises net worth for a chosen investment return and risk |
| Mortgage-free target | Overpayment needed to clear the mortgage in X years, and ERC risk |
| Monthly budget | Council tax by London borough, energy, running costs and spending; surplus feeds the overpay planner |
| Remortgage & moving | Switching, porting and equity |

Plus: theme switcher (auto, light, dark), copy-a-share-link, export/import of your plan as a file, print.

## Data sources
HMRC / GOV.UK (Stamp Duty Land Tax from 1 April 2025, Income Tax, National Insurance), Student Loans Company thresholds, GOV.UK Lifetime ISA rules, 2026/27 council tax Band D figures, Bank of England Bank Rate. Mortgage rates, fees, income multiples, running costs and investment returns are **illustrative assumptions** you should replace with your own quotes. Full credits are in the tool's About section.

## Privacy
No network requests at all. Inputs are stored only in your browser. A share link contains your inputs, so only send it to people you trust.

## Limitations
- Stamp duty is for England and Northern Ireland only (not Scotland's LBTT or Wales's LTT).
- Tax figures last checked **October 2026**; they change at Budgets and each April.
- Lender affordability is an approximation; every lender has its own criteria.
