# Piano Chords Practice: Major, Minor & 72 Inversions

A free, single-file workbook for learning major and minor triads and their inversions, then practising all 72 combinations.

## What it does
- Plain-English definitions: note, root, triad, major, minor, inversion, semitone.
- How to build major (0+4+7) and minor (0+3+7) triads and their three positions, with a one-octave key diagram.
- Table of all 72 combinations (12 roots × major/minor × 3 positions) with filters, search and a saved "practised" checklist and progress bar.
- A 15-minute daily routine.
- Self-test: random chord-and-position prompts with reveal, plus concept questions.
- Print-friendly; theme switcher (auto, light, dark).

## Sources and credits
Standard Western music theory. Inspired by online piano lessons on chords and inversions, including Jason Zachariah's "72 chords" lessons; not affiliated or endorsed. All text and code are original.

## Privacy
No network requests. Ticks are saved only in your browser.
