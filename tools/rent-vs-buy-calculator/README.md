# Rent vs Buy Calculator (UK)

A free, single-file comparison of buying a home against renting and investing the difference.

## What it does
- **Buying**: deposit, stamp duty (England & NI), fees, mortgage (fixed then remortgage), leasehold costs, insurance, maintenance, selling costs, house-price growth.
- **Renting**: rent and rent rises, insurance, deposit, moving costs; the difference invested in an ISA or general account.
- Net-worth comparison over your chosen horizon, break-even year, sensitivity ranges and a Monte Carlo view.
- Share link, export/import, print, theme switcher (auto, light, dark).

## Data sources
HMRC SDLT rates (from 1 April 2025) and 2026/27 tax. Market growth, rents, rates and returns are editable assumptions. Full credits are in the About section.

## Privacy
No network requests. Inputs are stored only in your browser.

Illustrations only, not financial, mortgage or property advice.
