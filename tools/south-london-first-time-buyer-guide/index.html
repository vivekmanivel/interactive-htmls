Stamp Duty Land Tax (England)

Rates in force since 1 April 2025: 0% to £125k, 2% to £250k, 5% to £925k, 10% to £1.5m, 12% above. First-time buyer relief: 0% to £300k and 5% to £500k, lost completely above £500k. Check GOV.UK before exchange.

Your privacy

Usualy Labs collects nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, cookies, trackers or forms that send data to us. Anything you save (shortlist, settings, imported files, an optional EPC API key) stays in your browser's local storage on your device; clear it at any time from your browser settings.

When you run a lookup, your browser requests data directly from the public services listed below, sending only what that request needs (for example a postcode or map coordinates). Those services have their own privacy policies. Links to other websites open those sites; we are not responsible for their content.
