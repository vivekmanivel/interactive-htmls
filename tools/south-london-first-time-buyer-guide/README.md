# South London First-Time Buyer Guide & Mortgage Planner

A free, single-file interactive tool for people buying their first home in South London.

**Open it:** `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages link on the main README). No install, no account.

## What it does
| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Property check | Enter a postcode to see nearby sold prices, schools (incl. grammars), crime, flood risk, planning, transport and EPC data on a map |
| Mortgage | Repayments, affordability and rate scenarios |
| Costs & stamp duty | SDLT (incl. first-time buyer relief) and total buying costs |
| Sold prices | Search Land Registry sales by postcode, sector, district or street |
| Shortlist | Save and compare properties (stored only in your browser) |
| Checklist & guides | Step-by-step buying checklist, LISA and leasehold notes |
| Offline data | Import official CSVs so lookups work without internet |
| About & sources | Data sources, credits, privacy and disclaimer |

## Data sources
See the **About & sources** tab. All data comes from official open-data publishers and OpenStreetMap; credit belongs to them.

## Privacy
Nothing is collected by Usualy Labs. Lookups go from your browser straight to the public services named in the tool. Saved items stay in your browser's local storage.

## Limitations
- Rates and thresholds were last checked **October 2026**. They change; always confirm on GOV.UK.
- Coverage is designed for South London boroughs.
- Some live services may be unavailable or rate-limited; the Offline data tab is the fallback.
- The EPC live lookup needs your own free API key, kept only in your browser.

## Disclaimer
Information only, not financial, mortgage, tax, legal or property advice. Provided as is, with no liability accepted. See the tool's About tab.
