Data sources and credits

All credit for the underlying information belongs to these publishers; inclusion does not imply their endorsement.

  • Payment method roles by market are indicative, compiled from publicly available scheme information, central bank and regulator publications and public market reports. Markets change quickly.
  • Return and reason codes: Pay.UK / Bacs (ARUDD, AUDDIS), European Payments Council SEPA rulebooks and ISO 20022 reason codes, Nacha (ACH return codes), ISO 8583 card response codes, as publicly documented.
  • Domestic scheme rules are summarised from public information by their operators and regulators, including NPCI (UPI), Banco Central do Brasil (Pix), Reserve Bank of India (e-mandates) and the card schemes.
  • Approval rates, fees, settlement times and dispute rates are illustrative assumptions, not data from any provider or merchant.
