# Payment Methods Explorer

A free, single-file guide to how people pay around the world, for anyone selling online or running subscriptions.

## What it does
- **33 markets** across four regions: which methods are dominant, major, common or emerging.
- **Method profiles**: cards, wallets, real-time payments (Pix, UPI and others), direct debit (Bacs, SEPA, ACH), BNPL, bank transfer, mobile money, cash vouchers; refunds, disputes, recurring support, settlement times.
- **Mix planner**: pick markets and see the coverage of your method set, with subscription gaps flagged.
- **Outcome model**: approvals, soft and hard declines, retries and recovery using indicative rates.
- **Code lookup**: card response codes, Bacs ARUDD/AUDDIS, SEPA and ACH return codes; glossary.
- Share link, export/import, print, theme switcher (auto, light, dark).

## Data sources
Indicative, compiled from public scheme, central bank and regulator information and public market reports; reason codes from Pay.UK/Bacs, EPC SEPA rulebooks, Nacha and ISO standards. Rates and fees are illustrative assumptions, not data from any provider. Full credits are in the About section.

## Privacy
No network requests. Inputs are stored only in your browser.
